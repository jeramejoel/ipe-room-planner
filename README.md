# Ipe Room Planner

Interactive 3D plan for a black music room: floating ipe slat walls with back-lit LEDs,
a double-walled control / vocal booth with a long double window and a sound-lock door,
draped fabric ceiling and layered rugs.

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages link. three.js is vendored
under `vendor/`, so once you have the folder it works with no connection, which is the
point of a tool you open at the lumberyard.

- Tap a wall chip for a flat elevation and its cut sheet
- Controls: panel heights, lighting scenes, LED color and dimmer, diffuser and absorber swaps
- Build guide: order of work, materials, and step-by-step builds for every piece

Where the numbers come from:

- Measured on site 2026-11-15: the north wall at 239", and the overhang at 65" wide,
  16" deep, underside 5' 0".
- Still assumed: stud positions, the obstacle positions, and the 97" ipe stock length the
  whole buy is packed onto. The board length is a control in the panel now, with a
  confirmed / assumed tag beside it. Tap 8 ft to see the cost of getting it wrong: at a
  true 96" the main band cannot be cut at all, and the buy goes from 119 boards to 210.
  Confirm with the supplier, then tick the box.
